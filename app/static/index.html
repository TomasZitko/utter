Utter*

A personal language for people whose speech changed after a stroke, Parkinson's, ALS or cerebral palsy. Record a sound three times, tell Utter what it means, and it speaks the full sentence for you.

Tap once, never hold. Recording stops by itself.
Nothing is spoken until the user confirms the guess. When Utter isn't sure, it asks again instead of guessing.
Works with one switch: the Space key starts and stops listening.
Yes, No and Help are always one tap away.
Small open model (WavLM), runs on this computer. Your voice never leaves it.
