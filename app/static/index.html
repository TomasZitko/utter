A personal language for people whose speech changed after a stroke, Parkinson's, ALS or cerebral palsy. Record a sound three times, tell Utter what it means, and it speaks the full sentence for you. When it isn't sure, it asks again instead of guessing.

Utter app · Veritas AI Hackathon 2026. Tested on the TORGO database of dysarthric speech (Rudzicz, Namasivam & Wolff, 2012). Everything runs on this computer, no voice leaves it.
